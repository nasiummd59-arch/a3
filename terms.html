1. Acceptance of Enrollment Covenants

These Terms of Academic Enrollment ("Terms", "Agreement") constitute a legally binding contractual covenant between you ("Student", "Scholar", "User", "You") and SmartCourseInk Institute of Digital Pedagogy ("SmartCourseInk", "the Academy", "We", "Us"). By creating an academic profile, accessing digital syllabus modules, participating in online colloquiums, or completing fee registration, you affirmatively agree to be bound by every provision articulated herein.

If you do not concur with these covenants, you must immediately refrain from creating an account or accessing any SmartCourseInk educational materials and platforms. Your continued usage of our services constitutes continuous acceptance of any periodic revisions made to these institutional terms.

2. Academic Honor Code & Scholarly Integrity

SmartCourseInk is dedicated to genuine intellectual growth, cognitive mastery, and ethical scholarship. Every enrolled pupil agrees to uphold our Academic Honor Code:

  • Originality of Submissions: All submitted problem sets, Socratic reflections, coding assignments, and capstone essays must represent your personal intellectual work. Plagiarism in any form will result in immediate disciplinary review.
  • Prohibition of Unauthorized Assistance: Pupils may not distribute exam answer keys, share unreleased assessment prompts, or utilize unapproved algorithmic shortcuts designed to bypass learning milestones.
  • Respectful Peer Colloquium Conduct: Scholars must conduct all forum discussions, peer review sessions, and faculty interactions with collegial respect, intellectual curiosity, and professional decorum.
  • Attribution Standards: Whenever citing external research, open-source libraries, or published theories, students must provide accurate academic citations in accordance with APA or IEEE standards.

3. Enrollment, Tuition Fees & Cancellation Governance

Enrollment in SmartCourseInk modular programs and specialized tracks is subject to space availability and prerequisite validation. All tuition fees and academic program dues must be settled in full prior to the release of syllabus access credentials.

Institutional Refund Policy: We offer a 14-day satisfaction guarantee for self-paced modular tracks. If within fourteen calendar days of enrollment you determine that the pedagogical framework does not align with your educational goals, you may submit a written refund petition to admissions@smartcourseink.com. Refund petitions are evaluated provided that less than thirty percent (30%) of the structured coursework has been marked completed.

Cohort-based executive seminars with assigned faculty mentors feature dedicated enrollment deadlines and may be subject to non-refundable administrative seat deposits as disclosed during registration.

4. Intellectual Authorship & Courseware Rights

All curriculum designs, lecture monographs, interactive widgets, spaced repetition algorithms, audiovisual recordings, diagrams, and written educational resources published by SmartCourseInk represent the exclusive intellectual assets of SmartCourseInk and its contributing faculty members. These materials are protected under international copyright, trademark, and intellectual authorship conventions.

Enrolled scholars are granted a limited, personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to view, read, and execute practice assignments for personal educational development. You may not republish, distribute, broadcast, commercially exploit, or mirror our curriculum assets on external websites or repositories without written institutional authorization.

Conversely, all intellectual creations, original codebases, and capstone projects developed independently by students during their course of study remain the exclusive intellectual asset of the student author.

5. Platform Usage Covenants & Security Conduct

When utilizing the SmartCourseInk web portals, discussion boards, and learning engines, you covenant that you shall not:

  1. Share your individual account access credentials or multi-factor security tokens with third parties.
  2. Deploy automated scrapers, web crawlers, data harvesting bots, or vulnerability testing tools against our digital infrastructure.
  3. Transmit malicious software, viruses, Trojan payloads, or corrupted code through our submission portals.
  4. Harass, intimidate, stalk, or discriminate against fellow scholars, teaching assistants, or faculty members.
  5. Impersonate another scholar, instructor, or institutional administrator in forum discussions or official correspondences.

6. Certification & Credential Verification Standards

SmartCourseInk digital certificates of completion, academic badges, and mastery transcripts are conferred only upon scholars who satisfy all published evaluation criteria, meet minimum attendance or engagement metrics, and attain passing scores across all required module assessments. The Academy reserves the right to withhold certification if academic integrity violations are uncovered during post-course audits.

Verified certificates are recorded on tamper-evident digital verification ledgers and can be shared with potential employers or academic institutions via unique verification URLs.

7. Suspension, Expulsion & Termination

SmartCourseInk reserves the right to suspend or permanently revoke access to all courses, student forums, and credentials without refund in the event of egregious breaches of our Academic Honor Code, harassment of academy personnel, fraudulent billing actions, or unauthorized distribution of proprietary educational assets.

Upon termination, all active licenses granted under these Terms immediately cease, and the affected user must discontinue all access to institutional course portals.

While our pedagogical architectures are constructed upon rigorous scientific learning research, we do not guarantee specific employment outcomes, salary augmentations, or academic admission into external collegiate universities.

9. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution Framework

These Terms and any dispute or claim arising out of your enrollment with SmartCourseInk shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and the United States of America, without giving effect to any principles of conflicts of law.
