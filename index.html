Frequently Answered

Curriculum & Admissions Inquiries

Essential information regarding cohort admissions, micro-credentialing accreditation, time commitments, and prerequisites.

How does the cohort admission and diagnostic assessment work?

Prospective scholars submit an academic application through our admissions concierge desk. Upon review, you complete a dynamic 45-minute diagnostic evaluation that assesses foundational logic and domain prerequisites, allowing our faculty to place you into the optimal cohort track.

What is the weekly time commitment required for intensive cohorts?

Intensive mastery cohorts require approximately 8 to 12 hours of dedicated deep focus per week, encompassing micro-modular syllabus review, hands-on sandboxed problem sets, and a weekly 90-minute live Socratic colloquium pod.

Are SmartCourseInk micro-credentials recognized by academic and industry bodies?

Yes. Our micro-credentials adhere to Open Badges 3.0 and IEEE learning standards, backed by verifiable cryptographic proofs of completed capstone projects and faculty reviews, widely recognized across leading technology firms and research universities.

Can I access course materials and spaced repetition decks after graduation?

Graduates enjoy lifetime access to our digital monograph archive, ongoing algorithm updates, and our continuous spaced retrieval flashcard algorithms to ensure lifetime knowledge retention.
